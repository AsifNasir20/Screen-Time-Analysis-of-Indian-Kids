# 📱 Screen Time Analysis of Indian Kids | 2025

A data-driven **SQL & Excel analysis** exploring digital exposure, screen usage patterns, device preferences, and potential health impacts among **Indian children aged 8–18**, based on **9,712 records**.

## 🔎 Key Analysis

* ⏱️ **Screen Time Patterns** — Analyzed daily screen exposure across children aged 8–18.
* 📱 **Device Usage** — Compared smartphones and other digital devices.
* 💤 **Health Impact Analysis** — Examined relationships between screen usage and reported health issues.
* 📚 **Educational vs Recreational Usage** — Compared time spent on educational and entertainment activities.
* 🏡 **Urban vs Rural Analysis** — Explored differences in screen-time behavior across locations.
* 👧 **Age & Gender Analysis** — Examined variations across age groups and gender.

## 💡 Key Insights

* ⏱️ **85% of children** in the dataset exceeded the 2-hour daily screen-time threshold.
* 📱 **Smartphones** were the most-used device category.
* 💤 **Poor sleep** emerged as the most commonly observed health issue in the analysis.
* 📉 The **Education-to-Recreation ratio was below 0.4**, indicating that recreational use was substantially higher than educational use.
* 🏡 **Rural children** recorded slightly higher average screen time than urban children.
* 👧 **Girls aged 14–16 in rural areas** represented a segment with comparatively high screen exposure and lower educational usage.

## 🧠 Analytical Framework

**Data Exploration → SQL Analysis → Excel Visualization → Pattern Identification → Insight Generation**

The project demonstrates how SQL and Excel can be combined to transform a large dataset into **clear, evidence-based insights around digital behavior and well-being**.
